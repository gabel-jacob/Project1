featured project

GopherParkLive

A personalized campus parking assistant designed through UX research, accessibility testing, prototyping, and user-centered interaction design.

Figma UX Research Accessibility Prototyping

Problem

Students struggle to predict parking availability during high-demand campus hours, leading to wasted time, congestion, and stress.

Solution

GopherParkLive provides real-time parking visibility, route guidance, and predictive congestion insights through an accessible mobile-first experience.

Process

The project included user interviews, journey mapping, wireframing, high-fidelity prototyping, accessibility testing, and iterative usability refinement.
