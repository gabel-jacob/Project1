about

I’m Jacob Gabel, a digital product designer and technical maker based in Minneapolis. I create accessible, user-centered digital experiences through UX research, frontend development, systems thinking, and physical prototyping.

My background combines Computer Science, UI/UX Design, and Digital Fabrication. I work across Figma prototypes, responsive websites, usability testing, design systems, CNC fabrication, and custom maker projects.

I’m especially interested in projects that connect thoughtful interface design with technical implementation — from campus apps and portfolio systems to custom wood keyboards and fabrication-based product experiments.
