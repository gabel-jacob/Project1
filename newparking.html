GopherParkLive

A campus parking assistant concept focused on reducing parking uncertainty through research, prototyping, usability testing, and iterative interface design.

UX Research User Testing Figma Accessibility Product Strategy

Problem

Students and staff often need to make parking decisions under time pressure, especially around class schedules, events, and peak demand.

Research

The project focused on understanding how users evaluate parking options, what information they trust, and how alerts or recommendations could reduce stress.

Testing

User testing helped evaluate task flow, label clarity, feature discoverability, and whether the interface communicated parking availability quickly enough.
