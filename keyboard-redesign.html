Walnut Keyboard Redesign

A second keyboard case project focused on improving the original design through stronger material choices, tolerance refinement, better internal clearance, improved gasket mount geometry, and a more intentional nature-inspired exterior.

Walnut Rhino RhinoCAM CNC Tolerance Testing

Why redesign?

After finishing the first wooden keyboard case, I identified several areas to improve: weak magnets, case fit, chamfer cleanup, PCB room, gasket mount height, and stronger material selection.

Design changes

The redesign explored walnut, a more refined case profile, layered mountain details, sun/moon pattern ideas, possible threaded inserts, and better alignment between the top and bottom halves.

Prototype findings

The first CNC prototype revealed tolerance issues that required sanding and dimensional updates. That failure became the key learning moment: physical prototyping made the problems obvious in a way that CAD alone could not.

Final direction

I refined the Rhino files, converted the project through RhinoCAM into G-code, and cut the walnut case on a CNC router. Walnut performed better than expected with minimal chipping, making it a stronger direction than the earlier material.

Role

Concept refinement, CAD redesign, CNC preparation, tolerance testing, material selection, and documentation.
